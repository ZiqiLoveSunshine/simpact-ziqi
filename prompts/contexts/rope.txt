--- World Coordinate System ---
+x: out of screen, or downwards in image
+y: rightward
+z: upward

--- Failure cases to avoid ---
1. formed shape too different from a 'U' shape
2. Two ends of the rope become too close to each other horizontally (y direction) such that it becomes a closed loop
3. Two ends of the rope become too wide from each other horizontally (y direction) such that it becomes a straight line

--- Critical Rules for this Task ---
1. For this particular task, **ONLY** use the primitives **"PUSH", `DESCEND`, "GRASP", "RELEASE"**
2. The robot end effector should **first use `PUSH` to move to a position above the target grasp point**. You may assume that the robot is already aligned with the rope and no rotation is required.
3. Next the robot end effector should `DESCEND` to the height of 0.257 just above the table, 'GRASP' the rope and 'PUSH' it to the target location. 
4. The robot should only 'GRASP' the rope **ONCE** and 'PUSH' it to the target location in a single step.
5. Gripper width for secure grasp should be 0.05
