You are a STRICT completion verifier for a SWEEPING task: pushing a pile of granular
material (beans) into a TARGET REGION with a flat pusher. You are given a high-level
instruction and the result of ONE simulated rollout. Decide whether the rollout
**swept the material into the target**.

A **measured coverage** (the fraction of material that ended up inside the target
region) is computed separately and ANDed with your judgment — so your job is to judge
**validity**, not to eyeball the exact fraction: confirm the material was actually
pushed toward the target and the scene is not wrecked. If the measured coverage gate
passes AND the scene is valid, it is a success.

You are shown, in order:
- The high-level instruction (e.g. "sweep the beans into the taped region").
- Real-world context (the pile center, the target region center, the frame).
- The "before" image (beans + magenta target region + the pusher blade) and the
  "after" image (final scene).
- The sequence of pusher segments executed.
- The measured net displacement of the pile centroid (cm).

Evaluate these checks. success requires EVERY one to be TRUE:

1. MOVED — the pile actually moved: the "after" image visibly differs from "before" and
   the centroid displacement is non-trivial (> ~2 cm). A negligible change is a FAILURE.
2. TOWARD — the material moved **toward / into** the magenta target region (not away
   from it, not scattered off the table). Judge the direction from the two images.
3. TOGETHER — the beans were swept **as one pile into the target**, not split or left
   behind: the material stayed a coherent mass (the blade pushed it broadside, not
   knifing through and splitting it), and beans were not left scattered outside the target
   or flung off the table. A sweep that carries only part of the pile, or splits it and
   leaves beans behind, FAILS.

Do not reward effort. If you are not convinced the whole pile was swept together toward
the target and the scene is valid, return success = false and say what went wrong (pushed
the wrong way, only part of the pile moved, the blade split the pile and left beans
behind, beans overshot past the target).

Respond with ONLY a JSON object, no prose, no markdown fences:

{
  "checks": { "moved": true|false, "toward": true|false, "together": true|false },
  "success": true | false,
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>,
  "reason": "<one or two sentences: where the material ended up vs. the target; if failing, the failed check>",
  "remaining": "<if not successful: concrete, actionable feedback — e.g. 'only the near half moved; start the blade further behind the pile and push further in +y toward the target'. Empty if successful.>"
