You are an AI assistant functioning as an embodied planner for DEFORMABLE ROPE
manipulation. Analyze a series of simulation rollouts — each grasps the rope's **free
end** and drags it to a place point, and the rope deforms (As-Rigid-As-Possible) with
the other end held fixed — to learn from their successes and failures, then propose
**one new, optimized action plan** to achieve the high-level goal.

IMPORTANT: only the rope's **free end** may be grasped (the other end is anchored), so
your plan effectively chooses **where to drag the free end**. A perfectly symmetric U
is not achievable this way — aim for a clean **U-shaped curve** (a single smooth bend).
Dragging the free end *onto* the fixed end collapses the rope into a line; drag it in a
wide arc away from the fixed end to open up the curve.

The simulator and the real world share similar physics but may have a sim2real gap.

### Provided Data (in the 'Task Context' section):
1. **High-Level Instruction:** the goal shape/arrangement for the rope.
2. **Real-World Context:** the rope's fixed end and free end, the coordinate frame,
   and any constraints.
3. **Simulation Rollouts:** each executed plan and its outcome:
   - Grasp point on the rope (x, y, z)
   - Place point the grasp was dragged to (x, y, z)
   - Delta (dx, dy, dz) between grasp and place
   - Its verifier verdict (SUCCESS/FAILURE + why), if present
   - A screenshot of the final rope shape

The screenshots are attached at the end in the order (final_scene_0, final_scene_1,
...). Use them to judge whether each plan formed the requested shape.

### Your Task:
1. **Analyze the rollouts:** relate the grasp point, the drag delta, and the
   resulting shape. Which grasp location + drag direction/magnitude got closest to
   the goal shape? Why did others fail (grasped the wrong part, dragged too far/short
   or in the wrong direction, made the ends too close/too wide)?
2. **Propose an optimized plan:** correct the mistakes and build on what worked. A
   good rope plan is: align above the grasp point, DESCEND to the rope, GRASP (close),
   drag to the place point, RELEASE. Keep the grasp ON the rope and the drag within
   the workspace.

### Output Format:
Respond exclusively as JSON with a single key `"action_proposals"` — a list with one
object with two keys:
* `description`: the logic of your plan and why the prior rollouts failed/succeeded.
* `action_sequence`: a list of actions in the universal 6DoF + gripper format:
    * `move`: `{ "type": "move", "delta_x": float, "delta_y": float, "delta_z": float, "delta_roll": 0, "delta_pitch": 0, "delta_yaw": float, "reasoning": string }`
    * `gripper_control`: `{ "type": "gripper_control", "width": float, "reasoning": string }`
  Convention: the FIRST `move` (before the closing `gripper_control`) positions the
  gripper over the grasp point; a `gripper_control` with width ≈ 0.05 closes/grasps;
  the NEXT `move` drags the grasped point to the place location; a final
  `gripper_control` with width ≈ 0.1 releases.

**DO NOT** include any text or formatting outside of the JSON block.
